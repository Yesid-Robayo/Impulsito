Variants are for a product sold in sizes, colors or other options. Extras are what gets added to a line when selling, such as a sauce or packaging. Both are set up in the editor of a product that is already saved: on its detail, **Edit** and then **More details**.

## Create variants

1. In **More details**, **Variants**.
2. In **Option 1**, the name of the option, for example size, and its values with **Add value**. **Add another option** adds a second one, for example color.
3. **Combinations** shows every pairing of values: check the ones to create.
4. **Apply to all** copies **Price**, **Cost** and **Minimum stock** to all of them with **Apply**. After that, each variant can have its own price, cost, **Opening stock** and **Barcode**.
5. Create the variants with the button at the bottom.

Each variant is a product with its own stock, price and history. Its name joins the product's name with its values, such as `T-shirt · M · Red`, and it takes the product's category, unit and tax.

Before creating them, the product cannot have stock of its own: if it does, adjust it to zero first. Once they are created, the options are fixed: values and combinations can be added, but an option cannot be renamed and no other option can be added. A combo or a variant does not take variants.

## Sell a variant

A product with variants is not sold on its own: tapping it in **Sell** opens **Choose variant**, with one option per row. Values with no stock are struck through but can still be chosen. Once every option is picked, the line is added on its own.

In **Inventory**, the product shows the sum of its variants, and each variant has its own row.

## Extras

1. In **More details**, **Extras**.
2. **Add extra** searches the products: each extra is another product, which can also be sold on its own.
3. **Remove** takes one off the list. Each change is saved right away.

When selling, tap the product's line to see its **Extras** and check the ones it takes. Each extra charges its product's sale price, pays tax, has a cost and takes its stock. It goes under its line and follows its quantity: two units with an extra take two.

A product's own fields and tags are something else: they are explained in [categories and fields](help_categories_fields).

{app} records the internal management of the business. It does not replace official accounting or invoicing, and it does not issue tax documents.

- A {app} receipt is not an invoice. {app} is not certified by any authority and does not replace any system required by law.
- Tax figures are estimates.
- **Profit is not the money available.** Buying goods takes money out and does not lower profit.
- **It does not process payments.** The payment method is a record: whoever sells checks that the money arrived.
- **Points are not money.** They only give a discount.
- It is for one person and one phone.

## For whoever keeps the books

The **Management summary**, in **Numbers**, brings together the sales and payments, the purchases and expenses, the closing inventory and the customers and suppliers of a period, as a PDF, a CSV or a .zip file. It carries its notice: it is not an invoice, an accounting ledger or a tax return.

**Pending invoicing**, in **Business**, helps keep track of the sales that are recorded in another system: each one is marked **Pending**, **Recorded elsewhere** or **Not needed**, with its external reference. **Open link** opens that system in another app, only if it was set up and only when tapped. {app} does not connect to any authority.

When in doubt, ask whoever keeps the books.

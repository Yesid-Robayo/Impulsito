Each product has a base unit, the one the inventory uses: unit, kilogram, liter, meter or another one that is created. Some allow decimals, like weight, and others only whole quantities.

## Conversions

A conversion is for buying in one unit and selling in another, such as buying by the box and selling by the unit. For example, 1 box = 24 units. Each conversion says whether it is **For purchase**, **For sale** or both, and it can have its own **Price of this unit**.

When selling or buying in another unit, {app} keeps the stock and the cost in the base unit: buying 2 boxes adds 48 units.

## Changes

- The base unit does not change if the product already has movements.
- Changing a conversion only affects new documents: earlier ones keep their factor.
- A unit in use is not deleted: it is archived.

Units are managed in **Business**, in **Units and conversions**.

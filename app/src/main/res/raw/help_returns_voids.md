A recorded sale is never edited or deleted. If there was a mistake or the customer returns something, {app} records the opposite movement: the sale stays in the history and the numbers correct themselves. Everything starts in the sale details: in **Records**, **Sales**, tap the sale.

## Void a sale

**Void sale** asks for the reason, **Entry error**, **Customer return** or **Other reason**, and an optional note.

- The units return to inventory, dated today.
- The sale stops counting in the numbers for its month, even if it is voided later.
- Money that went into an account comes out with an opposite movement. What was left on credit is offset in the customer's account, and anything already paid on account stays as a credit balance.
- The points it earned and the points it redeemed are reversed.
- The sale stays in the history, marked as voided.

Right after recording, **Undo** does the same in one tap. **Void and correct** voids the sale and opens a new one with the same data to fix it. If the new one is not saved, the old one stays voided anyway.

## Return part of a sale

1. **Return products** opens **Sale return**. In **What is returned**, choose how much of each line. The maximum is what was sold minus what was already returned.
2. **Return to inventory** comes checked. Unchecked, that stock counts as lost.
3. In **How it is settled**: **Refund money**, with the account in **Paid from** if accounts are used; **Credit balance**, for a customer; or **Exchange for another**.
4. Tap **Record return**.

What is returned goes back at the cost it had in the sale, not today's. In a sale on credit, **Credit balance** comes selected and reduces what the customer owes. The return counts on the day it is recorded: the figures for the month of the sale do not change, and neither do the points.

With an active return, the sale can no longer be voided: **Return the rest** returns what is left.

## Exchange for another product

**Continue with the exchange** opens the sale with the credit from what was returned. Choose the new products, and **Record exchange** saves everything together. If the new items are worth more, the difference shows as **Receivable**; if less, as **To refund**. Leaving without recording saves nothing.

## Void a return

In the sale details, under **Returns**, tap the return and then **Void return**. Stock and the money or credit balance go back to how they were, and the return stays in the history. An exchange is undone by voiding the new sale first.

If the sale was **Recorded elsewhere** in another system, voiding it here does not change that record.

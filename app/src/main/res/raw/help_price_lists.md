A price list keeps different prices for one type of customer, such as wholesale buyers, without duplicating products. There is always a **General price list**: its prices are the products' sale prices.

## Create a list

In **Business**, **Price lists**, tap **New list**:

- **Name**: for example, wholesale.
- **Type**: **Fixed**, with a price entered for each product, or **Relative**, which calculates its prices from a **Base list** with an **Adjustment** in percent. With an adjustment of -10, a product that costs 100 in the base list ends up at 90.
- **Save list**.

In a fixed list, a product without its own price uses the general price and shows **General price**. In a relative list, each price shows as **Calculated**.

## Change a price

In the detail of a fixed list, tap a product, enter the **Price in this list** and tap **Save**. The **Resulting margin** shows while typing. **Remove own price** sends the product back to the general price. In the **General price list**, changing a price changes the product's sale price.

**Update prices in this list** changes many prices at once: by **Percent**, **Amount**, **Target margin** or **By exchange rate**.

Each change goes into the product's history, in the **Prices** tab of its detail. Past sales do not change: each one keeps the price it was sold at.

## Use the list when selling

- In the customer's detail, **Edit** → **Price list** assigns one. When that customer is chosen in a sale, the prices switch to their list.
- In **Sell**, inside the cart, **List** picks another one for that sale.

These options appear when there is more than one active list. In a list's ⋮ menu, **Archive** retires it: its customers go back to the **General price list** and it is no longer offered in new sales. **Reactivate** brings it back.

## Reference currency

**Reference currency**, in **Business**, helps to think about prices from another currency. It does not change the business currency: sales, costs and balances stay in it.

1. **Choose currency** and its **Decimals**.
2. **Record exchange rate**: the **Exchange rate**, the **Date** and a **Note** with where it came from. It is entered by hand: {app} does not download it. Each new rate is added to the **History** without erasing the earlier ones.
3. On each product, the **Reference price** keeps its price in that currency.
4. **Reprice with this rate** prepares the price update so it can be reviewed before it is applied. No price changes on its own.

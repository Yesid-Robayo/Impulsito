A sale is saved on the phone when **Record sale** is tapped, with or without internet.

## Step by step

1. Tap **Sell** on **Home**. **Sell by hand** is also in **Say**, the button at the bottom center.
2. Choose the products in **Best sellers**, or search for them by name or by code. Each tap adds one.
3. To change the quantity or the price of that line, or to give a discount, tap the line.
4. Choose the payment method, if it is not the usual one.
5. Tap **Record sale**. Stock goes down, and the sale appears in **Home** and in **Records**.

## More checkout options

**More checkout options** opens the full checkout: several payments, an adjustment to the sale, the tip if it is turned on, the credit balance, points or a sale on credit to a customer. More in [Payment methods and fees](help_payments_fees) and in [Sales on credit and payments on account](help_credit).

## If something went wrong

- Right after recording, **Undo** voids that sale.
- Later, void the sale from its details with **Void sale**. The units go back to inventory and the sale stops counting in the numbers, but it stays in the history, marked as voided. Nothing is deleted.
- **Put on hold** saves an unfinished sale to serve someone else, and it is picked up again from **Sales on hold**.

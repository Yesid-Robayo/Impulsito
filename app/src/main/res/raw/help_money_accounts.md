Money accounts show how much money there is and where: cash, bank, wallet or other. They are optional: without them, {app} works the same.

## How money moves

- Each payment method can go into an account: when selling, the money collected is added there.
- Paying an expense or a purchase from an account lowers it.
- **Transfer** moves money between accounts, and the total does not change.
- **Withdrawal**: money taken out for personal use. It is not an expense and does not lower the final profit.
- **Contribution**: the owner's own money put into the business. It is not a sale.
- **Other income**: money that does not come from sales. It is shown separately and is not part of the final profit.

What a card or a transfer pays out later appears as **To settle** until its date.

## Cash register

The cash register is optional, and it is used to count the cash of a shift.

1. **Open register** with the **Starting amount**.
2. Sell as usual.
3. **Close register**: count the cash, by hand or with **Count by denomination**. {app} compares the **Counted** amount with the **Expected** amount and shows the surplus or the shortage.

The difference stays in the **Register history**.

## Money and profit

**Money available** is not profit: buying goods takes money out without lowering profit, and a sale on credit adds profit before the money arrives.

{app} measures profit by what the goods sold cost, not by the money that came in.

## The words

- **Margin**: out of every 100 sold, what is left after paying for the product.
- **Sales profit**: what was sold minus what those goods cost.
- **Final profit**: sales profit minus expenses, fees, and damage and loss.
- **Damage and loss**: what left inventory without being sold: expired, damaged, lost or given away.

## An example

A product sells for 100 and cost 60: the sales profit is 40 and the margin is 40%. If in a month goods that cost 300 were sold for 500, the sales profit for the month is 200. With expenses of 120 and fees of 10, the final profit is 70.

## Good to know

- **Profit is not the money available.** Buying goods takes money out and does not lower profit: that cost counts when the goods are sold. Money is shown in **Money accounts**, if they are used.
- A product without a cost inflates the margin, and {app} marks it **No cost**.
- With tax included or added, the margin is calculated without the tax.
- In **Numbers**, choose the period, and **How it was calculated** shows the math step by step.

The region decides how the business's amounts, dates and weeks look. The language only changes the app's text. Both are chosen on first use and changed later in **Settings**, under **Region and language**.

## Regional settings

**Regional settings** brings together:

- **Business currency** and **Decimals**: the currency in which every amount is saved.
- **Symbol (optional)**: 1 to 8 characters. Leave it empty to use the usual currency symbol.
- **Cash rounding**: the step to which the part paid in cash is rounded, when there are no coins to give the exact amount. At zero, nothing is rounded, and other payment methods charge the exact amount.
- **Number and date format**: the separators and the order of the date. It does not depend on the app language.
- **Business time zone**: decides which day each record belongs to. When it changes, saved records keep their date and new ones use the chosen zone.
- **First day of the week**: where each week starts in **Numbers** and in calendars.
- **CSV separator**: the one used by **Export to CSV**.

**Preview** shows sample figures with what was chosen, not business data. Changes are saved with **Save changes**.

## The currency is locked

Currency and decimals lock with the first record with money. Each amount is saved in the currency's smallest unit: changing the decimals later would change the value of everything saved. The symbol, rounding and formats can still be edited.

If the wrong currency was chosen, **Correct the currency** replaces it with another one with the same decimals. Amounts are not converted: they stay the same, now in the new currency. A safety copy is saved on this phone before the change, and **Change currency** can only be tapped after checking **I understand saved amounts are not converted**.

## Language

**Language** changes the text right away, without touching the currency or the number format. **Same as the phone** follows the phone's language. If the app does not include that language, it appears in English.

## Theme and colors

In **Settings**, **Appearance**:

- **Theme**: **Automatic** follows the phone, and **Light** or **Dark** stay fixed.
- **Colors**: nine palettes, and **Indigo and mustard** is the **Default**. Tap one to apply it right away; the sheet stays open to compare. It does not change the theme or the app icon.

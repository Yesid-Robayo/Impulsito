A quote offers prices to a customer, and an order follows a request until it is delivered. Neither is a sale: stock and the numbers change only when the sale is recorded. Both are in **Business** and, once the first one exists, also in **Records**.

## Quotes

1. In **Quotes**, tap **New quote**. With a sale already put together, ⋮ → **Save as quote** takes it with its products.
2. Choose the products as in a sale and tap **Save quote**. A **Customer**, **Valid until** and a **Note** can be added, all optional.
3. In the details, **Convert to sale** opens the sale with the products and the customer. If a price changed, choose **Use the quote price** or **Use the current price**.
4. When the sale is recorded, the quote becomes **Converted**. Leaving without recording keeps it open.

A quote past its date shows as **Expired** and can still be converted. Its menu has **Edit**, **Share quote**, which prepares a PDF with no costs or profits, and **Cancel quote**, which keeps it in the history but it can no longer be converted.

## Orders

1. In **Orders**, tap **New order**, or ⋮ → **Save as order** from a sale.
2. **Save order** asks, all optional, for the **Customer**, **Delivery date**, **Delivery address**, **Delivery note** and **Shipping charge**.
3. The order moves from **Pending** to **Ready** with **Mark as ready**, and to **Delivered** with **Record delivery**. From **Ready**, the menu offers **Move back to pending**.

- Each line shows what is **Reserved** and warns if stock is short, without blocking anything. Reserving does not move stock: it goes down only on delivery.
- **Record deposit**, with a customer chosen, saves an advance payment as that customer's credit balance. The summary shows the **Deposit** and what is **Due on delivery**.
- **Record delivery** opens the sale with the products, the customer and the shipping, and the deposit covers its part. Once it is recorded, the order becomes **Delivered**.
- When the date has passed, the row says **Delivery overdue**. The status does not change on its own.
- **Cancel order** stops reserving the units, and any deposit stays as the customer's credit balance.

A quote or an order cannot include extras: add them as separate products.

## Sales recorded in another system

{app} does not issue tax documents or connect to any authority. **Pending invoicing**, in **Business**, only helps keep track of the sales that are recorded in another system: each one is marked **Pending**, **Recorded elsewhere** or **Not needed**, with its **External reference** if needed.

**What data exists and where it goes** explains, in plain words, what {app} keeps, where each thing goes and how everything stands right now. It shows the real state read from the phone, not a promise, and it shows no business figures.

Open it in **Settings**, under **Business data**. Also from the welcome screen and from **Where to save backups**, before choosing a destination.

## What it shows

- **Right now**: where backups go, whether backup encryption is on, whether there is a second destination, whether the app lock is on and whether the version check is on. A value that could not be read says so, and an older one is never shown.
- **What data exists**: the **Business** data, the **Customers** and suppliers data, the **Photos** and the **Technical** data, such as the version and the phone model.
- **Where it goes**: exactly the app's network destinations, each with what it is for, what it sends and how to turn it off. There are no others.
- **What is not here**: no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no {app} servers.
- **Other apps**: when a link is tapped or a file is shared, another app does that work under its own rules.
- **Policy and terms**: the privacy policy and the terms of use.

## If the policy changed

If an earlier policy was accepted, a notice appears at the top with **Accept the new policy** or **Decide later**. Until it is accepted, cloud backups stay paused. Data, sales and exports keep working as usual.

To protect the account that backups depend on, see [the cloud account](help_cloud_account).

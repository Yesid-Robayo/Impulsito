Stock is a history of movements: each purchase, sale, adjustment or return adds or subtracts, and nothing is edited or deleted. If something was wrong, it is offset with another movement.

## Inventory

**Inventory** shows the stock of each product and what is **Running low**. With **Minimum stock** set on a product, {app} alerts when it is running out, if **Alert when a product is running low** is turned on in **Settings**.

If stock goes negative, the product appears in **Negative stock**: usually a purchase has not been recorded yet.

## Adjust stock

**Adjust stock** records an **In**, an **Out** or the **Actual count**, with its reason: **Damaged or broken**, **Expired**, **Lost or stolen**, **Own use**, **Gift or sample**, **Count correction** or **Other**. What leaves without being sold counts as damage and loss and lowers the final profit.

## Physical stock count

To count everything or part of it:

1. **New count**, by location or by category. With **Blind count**, the expected number is hidden, so whoever counts is not swayed by it.
2. Count each product. The count can be left and continued later.
3. **Review differences** shows each surplus and each shortage.
4. **Apply adjustments**: each difference becomes an adjustment in the history.

**Cancel count** applies nothing, and the stock does not change.

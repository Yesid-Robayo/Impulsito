**Numbers** shows how much was sold, how much was collected and how much was earned in a period. Everything is calculated on the phone from what was recorded.

## The period

- **Day**, **Week** or **Month**, with the arrows to go to the previous or the next one.
- In the ⋮ menu, **Choose dates** sets a custom range with **From** and **To**.

## The figure and the chart

The chips choose what to look at:

- **Sales**: what was sold, with how many sales there were and the **Average sale**.
- **Collected**: the money that came in. Below it, separately, **Sold**, **Payments on account** and **Receivable**, which is what all customers owe today.
- **Profit**: the **Sales profit**, what was sold minus what it cost.

Under the figure is the change against the previous period; a day is compared with the same day of the previous week. The chart has one bar per day: tap one to see its value, and **View as list** shows the same days as rows.

Selling more is not always earning more: a large sale with a thin margin can leave less than a small one. The notices under the chart say whether there are sales with products without a cost, products with negative stock, or whether the period changed because of voids or backdated records.

## Further down

- **How it was calculated**: from sales to **Final profit**, step by step. [Margin and profit](help_margin_profit) explains it.
- **Top sellers**, **By sales**, **By profit** or **By quantity**.
- **By payment method**, with fee and net, and **Expenses by category**.
- **Compare**, with **Month** chosen: against the previous month, in **Normal** or **Real**. [Price index](help_price_index) explains it.
- When they apply: **Profit at replacement cost**, **Projected cash flow**, **Break-even point**, **Analysis**, **Estimated tax** and **Other income**.

## Management summary

In the ⋮ menu of **Numbers**, or in **Business**, **Management summary** prepares a file for whoever keeps the books:

1. Choose the month with the arrows, or **Choose dates**.
2. In **What it includes**, the **Summary PDF** always goes, and **Sales and payments**, **Purchases and expenses**, **Closing inventory** and **Customers and suppliers** are optional.
3. **Generate summary**. With the app lock on, the phone asks to confirm the identity.
4. In **Summary ready**, **Share** or **Save to a folder**.

The file is an unencrypted .zip with sales, costs and profit: share it only with the right person. It is supporting material, not an accounting book or a tax return.

An expense is what it costs to run the business without being merchandise to sell: rent, utilities, transport. Recording it shows the real profit. Merchandise goes in as a purchase, not as an expense.

## Record an expense

1. In **Home**, tap **Expense**, or in **Records**, **Expenses**, tap **New expense**.
2. Enter the **Amount** and choose the **Category**. If no other is chosen, it stays **Other**, and **New category** creates one without leaving.
3. **Description** is optional. **Date**: today or an earlier one, never a future one.
4. If money accounts are used, choose the **Money account** it comes from. Without an account, the expense is still recorded and moves no account.
5. Tap **Save expense**.

**Expenses** shows one month at a time, with its **Month total**, and can be filtered by **Category**.

## Void or correct

An expense is not edited. Tapping it opens its sheet:

- **Void and correct** voids the expense and opens a new one with the same data.
- **Void expense** asks for the reason, **Entry error** or **Other reason**, and an optional **Note**. The expense is crossed out and stops counting in the numbers for its date.

## Expense categories

In **Settings**, **Categories**, the **Expenses** tab lets you **Rename**, **Delete** a category with no expenses, or **Archive** one that has some. An archived category stops appearing in new expenses and stays in the numbers. **Other** cannot be deleted or archived.

**Fixed expense** marks the categories that do not depend on how much is sold, like rent: with them, **Numbers** calculates the **Break-even point**.

## Recurring expenses

For what repeats, in **Business**, **Recurring expenses**, or from the ⋮ menu in **Expenses**:

1. **New recurring expense**: **Amount**, **Category**, **Description** and **Repeats**: **Every week**, **Every month** or **Every year**, with its day and the **Next date**. If the month does not have that day, the last day is used.
2. With **Reminder**, an **Expense to confirm** alert arrives, without amounts.

A recurring expense is never recorded on its own. When its date comes, it appears in **To confirm**: **Record** opens the new expense with its data, which is saved with **Save expense**, and **Skip** records nothing and moves to the next date. **Archive** stops it from being proposed, without changing expenses already recorded.

## How they lower profit

Active expenses are subtracted on their date: **Final profit** is the sales profit minus expenses, fees, and damage and loss. More in [Margin and profit](help_margin_profit).

{app} comes with no taxes or rates from any country: each person enters their own in **Settings**, in **Taxes**.

## How tax is charged

- **No tax**: prices do not carry tax. This is the starting value.
- **Included**: the sale price already includes the tax, and {app} works out the base backwards.
- **Added**: the tax is added to the price at checkout.

With **Included** or **Added**, the margin is calculated without the tax. Changing the mode only affects new sales.

## Rates

Each rate has a **Tax name** and a **Percentage**. One can be set as **Default**, and each product can use another one. Each sale keeps the rate it was made with: changing the rate later does not change past sales.

With **Recoverable purchase tax** turned on, the tax on purchases is not part of the cost.

## What it is not

Tax figures are estimates, for internal management. They are not a tax return to any authority, and {app} does not issue tax documents. More in [What {app} does not cover](help_not_accounting).

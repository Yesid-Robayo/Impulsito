Categories, custom fields and locations organize the catalog without changing the stock or the business numbers.

## Categories

- They are managed in **Settings**, **Business profile**, **Categories**, with **New category**. The list goes by name.
- They can also be created while editing a product: in **Category**, type a name that does not exist and choose the option to create it.
- In each one's menu, **Rename** or **Delete**. When one is deleted, its products are left with **No category**: none is deleted.
- In **Products**, **Filter and sort** → **Category** shows only that category. A stock count, printable labels and the shareable catalog can also be made by category.

The **Expenses** tab on the same screen holds the expense categories.

## Custom fields

In **Business**, **Fields and tags**, **New field** adds a custom detail to each product, such as brand, warranty or size. Its **Type** is **Text**, **Number**, **Date** or **Yes or no**, and once any product has a value, the type does not change. **Move up** and **Move down** change the order in which they are asked for.

Fields are filled in while editing the product, under **More details**. A field with values is not deleted: it is archived, it is no longer asked for, and its values are kept and appear in exports.

## Locations

With more than one place, such as a store and a warehouse, in **Business**, **Locations and transfers**, **New location** adds each one.

- The product details show the **Stock by location**, and the inventory can be viewed by location or in **All locations**.
- Sales, purchases and adjustments say which location the stock leaves from or arrives at.
- **New transfer** moves stock from one location to another, and **Void transfer** moves it back.
- A count by location compares what was counted with what is in that location, not with the product's total.
- A location with stock is not archived: what it holds is transferred first.

## Archive instead of delete

A product with no movements can be deleted. One with movements is archived: in its details, ⋮ → **Archive**. It stops appearing when selling, its sales are kept, and it can be brought back with **Reactivate**. Archived products are shown in **Products**, with the **Archived** filter.

## What does not change

Once a product has movements, its base unit, its **Stock mode** and batch tracking do not change: the history was written that way, and changing them would throw off the stock and the cost. The form shows them read-only. More about units and conversions in [Units and conversions](help_units).

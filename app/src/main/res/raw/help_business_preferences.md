Some rows in **Settings** change how {app} suggests prices, sends alerts and charges. They are business data, and they go in backups like everything else.

## Target margin

In **Business profile**, **Target margin** is the margin you aim to earn on the price, from 0 to 99 %. It applies to products that have none of their own and get none from their category. It is used in two places:

- **Suggest price**, in the product editor, proposes the price that leaves that margin with the current cost.
- **Review**, in **Inventory**, flags **Margin below target** on products that earn less.

Changing it touches no price: it only changes what is suggested and what is flagged. The difference between margin and profit is explained in [margin and profit](help_margin_profit).

## Days until a cost is old

In **Alerts**, **Days until a cost is old** sets after how many days without buying a product its cost counts as old. **Review** then flags it, so you can check the cost is still real before setting the price. With 0 it is **Off**. Replacement cost is explained in [replacement cost](help_replacement_cost).

## Daily summary

In **Alerts**, **Daily summary** sends a notification from this phone every day, without internet, with the day's sales, total and sales profit. Turn it on with the switch and choose the time in **Summary time**, with **Save time**.

- With the phone locked, the notification shows no amounts.
- With discreet mode, it only says how many sales there were.
- It needs the app's notifications to be allowed. If they are not, **Settings** says so and leads to the phone's settings.
- It may arrive late.

## Tips

In **Sales and payment**, **Tips** adds the **Tip** field at checkout, with **No tip** to leave it at zero. The tip is added to what the customer pays, but it does not count as a sale or as profit. It does pay the fee of the payment method used. When off, the field does not appear.

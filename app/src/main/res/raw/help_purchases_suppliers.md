A purchase is merchandise coming into the business. It adds stock and updates the cost of each product, but it does not lower profit: that cost counts when the product is sold.

## Record a purchase

1. In **Home**, tap **Purchase**, or in **Records**, **Purchases**, tap **New purchase**. From a product's details, **Record purchase** opens it with that product.
2. Choose the **Supplier** or leave **No supplier**. If the product has a usual supplier, it is chosen for you.
3. **Date**: today or an earlier one. **Document reference** is optional.
4. **Add product** for each product. Each line takes the **Quantity** and, as preferred, the **Unit cost** or the **Line total**: the other one is calculated. A line at 0 counts as a free bonus.
5. In **Payment**, **Paid** or **On credit**. If money accounts are used, choose the **Money account** the payment comes from.
6. Tap **Record purchase**.

## What changes

- The stock of each product goes up.
- The average cost is recalculated, and each line shows how it changes. The calculation is in [Average cost and replacement cost](help_replacement_cost).
- Past sales and the sale price do not change. If a product falls below the target margin, **Change price** is offered.
- A cost very different from the previous one shows a warning, but it can still be recorded.

## Suppliers

In **Products**, **Suppliers**, **New supplier** asks for the name. Phone, email, notes and **Payment terms** are optional: the terms suggest the due date of purchases on credit.

The supplier details show the **Total purchased**, the **Usual products** and their **Purchases**. A supplier with purchases is not deleted: it is archived, stops appearing in new purchases, and its history is kept.

## Void or correct

A purchase is not edited. In its details:

- **Void and correct** voids the purchase and opens a new one with the same data.
- **Void purchase** asks for the reason. The units leave the inventory and the average cost is recalculated. What was sold does not change, and stock can go negative.
- A purchase with an active return cannot be voided.

## Return to the supplier

For bad merchandise from a real purchase, use **Return to supplier**, from the purchase, from the supplier, or in **Business**:

1. Choose the **Original purchase** or **No original purchase**, and how much of each product goes back.
2. In **How it is settled**, **Refund** or **Credit with supplier**, which lowers what is owed to them.
3. Tap **Record return**. What is returned leaves the stock at its cost.

**Void return**, in its details, reverses it. Products with batches cannot be returned here.

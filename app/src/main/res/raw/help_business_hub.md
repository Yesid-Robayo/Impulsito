**Business** gathers what is not used every day, so that **Home** and the tabs do not fill up. Open it with **Business**, on **Home**; on a tablet, also from the side rail. This is where the work happens; configuration is in **Settings**.

## How it is arranged

- **Search tools** filters by name and by the line that says what each one is for.
- **In use** shows the tools that already have their first record. It does not appear if there are none.
- **More tools** gathers the rest in folded groups, each one with how many it holds.
- At the end, **Help** opens this help.

Nothing is turned on or off by hand: a tool moves to **In use** with its first record, and what is not used stays one tap away.

To sell without looking for the app, {app} offers a widget for the phone's home screen, a tile for quick settings and shortcuts on its icon. None of them shows amounts unless asked.

## Add the widget

1. Touch and hold an empty space on the home screen and open the widget list. The phone names those menus, and they change by model.
2. Look for {app}: its widget says **Sell and check the day**.
3. Drag it to the home screen. It can be made larger or smaller.

## What it shows

- **Sell**, **Expense** and **Purchase**, which open the app on that task.
- **Today**, with how many sales there were and how many products are running low. These are counts, not amounts.
- **Backup failed**, if the backup failed. Tap it to open the backup.
- With no products, **Create the first product**.

At the smallest size only the buttons remain. If the database is not ready yet, only **Sell** remains. The widget follows the phone's light or dark mode, takes the **Colors** chosen in the app and writes its text in the app language.

## Show amounts

By default, the widget shows no amounts. At the tallest size an eye appears, **Show amounts**, which adds **Today's sales**. The choice belongs to that widget and does not travel in backups. Even when chosen, the amount is not shown while the phone is locked or with **Discreet mode** on. **Hide amounts** hides it again.

## The New sale tile

1. Open the phone's quick settings and start editing them.
2. Drag **New sale** to the panel.

Tap it to open a new sale. It never shows business data: no amounts, no counts, no names. With the phone locked, the app opens only after unlocking it.

## Icon shortcuts

Touch and hold the {app} icon to see **New sale**, **New expense** and **New purchase**.

## With the app lock

With **Lock with fingerprint or PIN** on, neither the widget, the tile nor the shortcuts skip the lock: the task waits until the app is unlocked.

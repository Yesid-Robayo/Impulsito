The **Price index** keeps how much prices changed in general each month, entered by hand. It shows whether selling more really means selling more, or whether prices just went up. {app} does not download it or bring it from anywhere.

## Enter the months

In **Business**, **Price index**, or in the ⋮ menu of **Numbers**:

1. **Add month**.
2. Choose the **Month**: the current one or an earlier one.
3. Enter the **Change for the month** in percent, with up to 2 decimals and a minus sign if prices went down. For example, 2 if they rose 2%.
4. Write down the **Source**: where the figure came from.
5. **Save month**.

Each month has a single value. To correct it, tap the month, change it and save. **Delete month** removes it, and the real comparison no longer covers it. In the ⋮ menu, **Import from a file** opens the data import.

## Compare in real terms

**View comparison in Numbers** opens **Numbers** with the comparison in **Real**. In **Compare**, with **Month** chosen:

- **Normal** compares what was sold in the month with what was sold in the previous month, as is.
- **Real** takes what was sold in the month to the previous month's prices, without the price rise, and shows its change.

An example: the previous month sold 100, this month 110, and this month's index says prices rose 10%. In **Normal**, sales grew 10%. In **Real** they stay at 100: the same was sold, at higher prices.

The real figure is an **Estimate**, not a recorded figure. If months of the index are missing, the screen says which ones and never assumes a value. With the current month, the comparison uses the same days of the previous month.

**Goals and limits** compares the month with a goal of your own and follows an income ceiling that the person writes. {app} does not suggest goals and knows no rule: the numbers belong to whoever writes them. It opens from **Business**, **Goals and limits**, or by tapping the **Monthly goal** card on **Home**.

## Goals for the month

1. Choose the month with the arrows. Past months can be opened, and upcoming ones can be set up to a year ahead.
2. **Set goals**: **Sales goal**, **Final profit goal** or both.
3. **Save goals**.

Each goal shows what was achieved, the goal and the percentage, and when it is reached it says **Goal reached**. Sales are counted as in **Numbers**, and final profit is the one in [Margin and profit](help_margin_profit). With a sales goal, **Home** shows the **Monthly goal** card.

**Edit goals** changes them. To remove them, leave both fields empty and save.

## Income limit

It helps to follow a ceiling the person knows, for example a yearly one.

1. **Set limit**.
2. Enter the **Limit amount**.
3. Choose the **Months included**, from 1 to 60. It includes the current month: with 12, it adds the current month and the 11 before it.
4. **Save limit**.

The bar shows the total for those months against the limit. The total is the sales of those months, without returns or voids. If the business has less history, it says from which date it adds up.

## The notices

- On reaching 80% and 95% of the limit, a notice appears on the screen and a notification is sent.
- When it is passed, the notice says **The written limit was exceeded**.

Each level gives notice only once, and dropping to a lower level gives none. The notification shows no amounts and only goes out if notifications for {app} are allowed. No notice ever blocks a sale.

**Edit limit** changes the amount or the months, and **Remove limit**, in the same sheet, removes it.

This limit is only a reference: {app} does not say whether any rule is met.

A sale on hold keeps what is being sold, to serve someone else and finish it later. It is not a sale: it does not change stock, money or reports, and it does not set units aside. Stock goes down only when the sale is recorded.

## Put on hold

1. In **New sale**, with at least one product, open ⋮ → **Put on hold**. It is also next to **Clear sale**.
2. If useful, type a **Name or reference**: a table or a name, up to 40 characters.
3. Tap **Save on hold**. The screen is left empty for the next sale.

It keeps the products, with their quantity, any price typed by hand, the discount on each line and their extras. Payments and the customer are chosen at checkout.

## Resume

- In the top bar of the sale, the **On hold** button shows how many there are. They are also in **Business**, in **Sales on hold**.
- Each row shows the name or **No name**, the products, how long it has been waiting and an estimated total at today's prices.
- Tap the row to **Resume**. If another sale is half done, **Switch sales?** puts it on hold and resumes the chosen one: neither is lost.
- On resuming, the current price is used, except one typed by hand, and {app} says how many prices changed. An archived product is left out, with a notice.
- It is removed on its own when the sale is recorded. Resuming it and leaving without recording loses nothing.
- The menu on each row offers **Rename** and **Discard**, which deletes only what was saved there.

## Discounts and surcharges

- On a line: tap the line and enter the **Discount** as an **Amount** or a **Percentage**.
- On the whole sale: in **More checkout options**, **Sale adjustment** applies a **Discount** or a **Surcharge**, **By amount** or **By percentage**, with **Apply adjustment**. **Remove adjustment** undoes it. The discount cannot exceed the total.
- A customer's points also give a discount: more in [Customers and points](help_customers_points).

## Extras

If a product has extras, such as an extra portion, a sauce or packaging, they appear in **Extras** when its line is tapped, and the ones it takes are marked. Each extra is another product: it adds its price and lowers its stock when sold. Set them up when editing a product that is already saved, in **Extras**, with **Add extra**. A quote or an order cannot include extras.

## Tips

Turn them on in **Settings**, in **Sales and payment**, with **Tips**. Then **More checkout options** shows **Tip**: it is added to what is charged and pays its method's fee, but it does not count in sales or in any profit. **No tip** removes it.

A receipt is the summary of a sale for the customer. It is not an invoice, and it never shows costs or profits.

## Share

From the details of a sale, share the **Receipt** with **Share PDF** or **Share image**, with the app of your choice. The file leaves the phone only when the person shares it.

## Template

In **Receipt template**, in **Settings**, choose the logo, the **Business name**, the **Business details** and the **Receipt footer**. The **Fixed notice**, which says that it is not an invoice, always appears and cannot be removed.

## Print

- With a Bluetooth thermal printer: first pair it in the phone settings, then add it in **Printers**, with the paper width, and test it with **Print test**.
- With a Wi-Fi or USB printer: **Print with the system** uses the Android print service.

{app} prints only on printers already paired with this phone, and only when **Print** is tapped.

## Other documents

Quotes, orders, a customer's statement and the catalog to share follow the same rules: no costs, no profits and never called an invoice.

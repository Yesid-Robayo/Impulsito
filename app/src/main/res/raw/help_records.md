**Records** gathers what has already been recorded, to find it by date and, if needed, void it. Nothing is edited: a wrong sale, purchase or expense is voided, and it stays in the history.

## The segments

At the top, choose **Sales**, **Purchases** or **Expenses**. **Quotes** and **Orders** also appear as soon as the first one exists: they are explained in [quotes and orders](help_quotes_orders). Switching segments keeps each one's filters.

## Sales and purchases

- **Period**, on the left, chooses **All dates**, a **Day**, a **Week** or a **Month**, with arrows below to move to the previous or next one.
- **Filters**, on the right, shows only one **Payment method** for sales or one **Supplier** for purchases, and **Voided only**. **Clear filters** keeps only the period.
- The list goes by day, with what was sold or bought that day. Each sale shows the time, the first product, the method or **Multiple payments**, and the total. Each purchase shows the supplier or **No supplier**, how many products and the total.
- What was voided is struck through and does not add to its day.

With discreet mode, purchase totals are hidden, because they are costs.

## Expenses

At the top are the month with its arrows, the month's total and the **Category** filter. **New expense** records one. Tap an expense to open its sheet, with **Void expense**: see [expenses](help_expenses).

## Sale detail

Tap a sale to open its detail: the total, the lines, the payments and what it left. Cost, profit and fees are hidden in discreet mode. At the end:

- **Return products**, or **Return the rest** if there was already a return.
- **Void and correct**: voids it and opens a new sale with the same data, to fix it. If the new one is not saved, the old one stays voided anyway.
- **Void sale**, with its reason.

In the menu, **Share** and **Receipt**. Returns and voids are explained in [returns and voids](help_returns_voids).

## Purchase detail

Tap a purchase to open its detail, with **Return to supplier**, **Void and correct** and **Void purchase**. If it was bought on credit and something is still owed, also **Record payment**. See [purchases and suppliers](help_purchases_suppliers).

On a wide screen, the detail opens on the right, without leaving the list.

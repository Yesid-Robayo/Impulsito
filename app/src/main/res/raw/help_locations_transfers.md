With more than one place, such as a store and a warehouse, {app} keeps the stock of each one separately. While there is only one location, none of this appears: everything goes to the initial location.

Open it in **Business**, **Locations and transfers**. Also from **Inventory** or **Suggested restock**: in the location picker, **Manage locations**.

## Create a location

In **Locations**, **New location** asks for **Name** and **Type**: **Store**, **Warehouse**, **Stall** or **Other**. Save it with **Save location**.

Tap a location to see how much of each product is there, in quantities only. Its menu lets you rename or archive it. A location with stock cannot be archived: transfer it to another one first. Once archived, it is no longer offered in sales, purchases and transfers, and its history is kept. The initial location cannot be archived.

## Stock in each place

With two locations or more, a location picker appears:

- In **Sell** and when adjusting stock, it chooses where stock leaves from or comes into.
- In a purchase, it chooses where the goods arrive.
- In **Inventory**, it shows one location or **All locations**.

A count is done per location, with what is expected in that place: see [stock and counts](help_stock_counts).

## Transfer between locations

1. In **Transfers**, **New transfer**. It only appears with two locations or more.
2. Choose **From** and **To**.
3. **Add product** shows what is in the origin. Enter the quantity of each one and, if the product tracks batches, the batch.
4. A **Note**, if needed.
5. **Record transfer**.

If a line would leave the origin negative, a warning shows what would be left, but it is not blocked. The transfer moves quantities without changing cost or profit.

**Transfers** lists the recorded ones by day. Tap one to see what it moved; **Void transfer** returns the stock to the origin. The transfer stays in the history, marked as voided.

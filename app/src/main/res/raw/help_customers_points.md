A customer is useful for selling on credit, tracking a balance and seeing who buys most. A sale does not need one.

## Create a customer

1. In **Business**, **Customers**, tap **New customer**.
2. **Choose from contacts** opens the phone's picker and brings the name and phone number of the chosen contact. {app} does not read the address book: it only receives that contact.
3. Only the **Name** is required. **Phone**, **Email** and **Notes** are optional: record only what is needed and do not write sensitive data.
4. Tap **Save customer**.

A repeated name gets a warning, and it can be saved anyway. **Price list** appears when there is more than one.

## Choose the customer when selling

In **More checkout options**, **Customer** shows the **Recent** ones, and **View customers** opens the full list, where one can also be created. From a customer's details, **Sell to this customer** opens the sale with them already chosen.

## The list and the history

- **Search customers** searches by name or phone, and the filters are **All**, **Receivable**, **Credit balance** and **Archived**.
- The details show the **Balance** and two tabs: **Movements**, with each sale on credit, payment on account, return and adjustment, and **Sales**, with each sale, which opens with a tap.
- Debts, payments on account and reminders: [Sales on credit and payments on account](help_credit).

## Points

1. In **Business**, **Points program**, turn on **Program active**.
2. Enter the **Amount to earn 1 point** and the **Discount per point**. An example shows how it works out.
3. Tap **Save program**.

- Each sale with a customer earns points on what was sold before tax, after any redemption. With 100 to earn 1 point, a sale of 1250 earns 12.
- To redeem, in **More checkout options**, with the customer chosen, **Points** shows those available. Enter the **Points to redeem** and tap **Redeem points**: it is a discount on the sale, never a payment.
- Points are not money: they cannot be exchanged for cash, are not a credit balance and do not count as debt.
- Changing the amounts does not affect what was already earned. Turning off the program keeps the balances.
- Voiding a sale reverses its points. A return does not change them.

## Archive and anonymize

In the details, ⋮:

- **Archive**: the customer no longer appears when selling, and the history is kept. If they owe, the balance still counts in **Receivable**. **Reactivate** brings them back.
- **Anonymize**: deletes the name, phone, email, notes and the delivery details of their orders, and this cannot be undone. Movements are kept, and the list shows them as **Anonymous customer**. That data stays in earlier backups until they age out through retention: **Update backups** asks for a new backup.

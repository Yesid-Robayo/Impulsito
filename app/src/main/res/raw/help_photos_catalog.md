A photo helps recognize the product when selling and can go in the catalog shared with customers. Photos are saved on the phone, outside the database.

## Add a photo

1. While editing the product, open **More details**.
2. In **Photo**, tap **Choose photo**. The phone's photo picker opens, and it asks for no permissions.
3. Choose the image and tap **Save product**.

{app} does not take photos with the camera: one already on the phone is chosen. The photo is saved lighter, without location or camera data, and the original is not copied. **Change photo** puts in another one and **Remove photo** takes it off. If an image cannot be read, choose another one.

## Photos and backups

Photos are not part of the data backup:

- With WebDAV, they upload separately to the same cloud, one by one and without holding back the data backups. If fewer than 3 backups fit in the service, photos stop uploading and the data backups continue.
- With the phone's cloud, photos do not leave the phone. They are saved with **Export photos**, in **Settings**, **Local backups**, in a separate ZIP file.
- The backup status shows how many photos are not backed up yet, and **All saved** does not include them.

When restoring on another phone with WebDAV, photos download after the data. One that is not in the cloud shows as **Photo unavailable**, with no data missing.

## Shareable catalog

In **Business**, **Shareable catalog** builds a PDF with products and prices to send to customers:

- **Which products**: **All** or one **Category**.
- **Price list**: the prices it shows.
- **Show photos**, **Group by category** and **Include out of stock**.
- **Title**: any title, or **Catalog**.

The screen says how many products and pages it has. The PDF is sent with **Share PDF** or saved with **Save PDF**.

## What it includes and what it does not

The catalog has the logo, the name and the details of the business from the **Receipt template**, each product with its unit and price, variants with their own, the date of the prices and a notice saying that it is not a sales receipt.

It never has costs, margins, profits, suppliers or stock. Archived products, inputs and products without a price in the chosen list do not go in either.

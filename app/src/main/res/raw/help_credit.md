A sale on credit is a sale that the customer pays later. It needs a customer: what is still owed goes to their account.

## Sell on credit

1. In **Sell**, open **More checkout options** and choose the customer.
2. Charge the rest with a method of type **On credit**, with its **Due date** if a date was agreed.
3. Record the sale. **Balance after this sale** shows how the account stands.

## Payments on account

In the customer details, **Record payment on account** records each payment: amount, payment method, money account and date. The balance goes down, and anything paid beyond it stays as a credit balance. A wrong payment is voided with **Void payment**: it is not deleted.

## The customer's account

- **Owes**, **Credit** or **Up to date** show where the balance stands, and **Overdue** shows that the date has passed.
- **Movements** shows each sale on credit, payment on account, return and adjustment, with the balance after each one.
- **Share statement** prepares a document with the movements, without costs or profits.
- **Send reminder** prepares a message to send from another app. {app} sends nothing on its own.

## Customer data

Only the name is required. Record only what is needed, and do not write sensitive data in the notes. **Anonymize** deletes the personal data and keeps the movements.

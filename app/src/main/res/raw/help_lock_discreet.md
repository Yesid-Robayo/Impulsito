## Lock

**Lock with fingerprint or PIN**, in **Settings**, asks for the phone's fingerprint or PIN when opening {app}. It stops someone else from opening the app, but it does not encrypt the data. **Ask again after** chooses when it asks again: **Immediately**, **1 minute** or **5 minutes**.

It needs a screen lock on the phone. Without one, anyone who picks up the phone can see the data, and **Settings** warns about it.

With the lock turned on, the most sensitive actions, such as exporting the data, ask for the fingerprint or PIN again.

## Discreet mode

With customers nearby, **Hide figures**, in the top bar, hides costs, profits, margins, fees, inventory value, balances, the cash register and totals. Prices and the total to charge stay visible. **Show figures** shows them again.

Discreet mode hides the screen, but it does not protect the files.

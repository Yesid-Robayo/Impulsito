Each product has a name, a sale price and, if known, its cost. With the cost, {app} calculates the margin and the profit of each sale. Without a cost, the sale is still recorded, but its profit is not real: the product is marked **No cost**.

## Create a product

- **Name**, **Category** and unit.
- **Sale price**: what is charged.
- Cost: what each unit cost. Purchases update it later.
- **Opening stock**: what there is today.
- **Minimum stock**: the number below which the product is running low.
- **Use**: **For sale**, **Input** or **Both**. An input does not appear when selling, but it is purchased, counted and used in recipes and production.

**Suggest price** proposes a price based on the cost and the target margin of the business profile, in **Settings**.

## Change the cost

A product with movements does not change its cost in **Edit**: the cost shows there, but purchases calculate it, as an average of what is in stock. To change it:

- If what you pay when buying went up, record the purchase in **New purchase** with the new cost, and the average moves on its own.
- If the cost is wrong or was never entered, **Correct cost**, below the cost in **Edit** or in the ⋮ menu of the details, sets the new cost from now on. Past sales keep their profit, and the inventory value changes.
- To know what it would cost to buy it today, without touching profit already recorded, use the **Replacement cost**: see [average cost and replacement cost](help_replacement_cost).

## Change prices

- A price change only affects new sales: past sales keep the price they were sold at.
- The bulk update changes many prices at once, by percentage and with rounding, and **Undo the last one** reverts it.

## Archive

A product with movements is not deleted: it is archived. It no longer appears when selling, and its history is kept.

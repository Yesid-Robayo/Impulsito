The cloud account where the backups go matters as much as the phone: anyone who gets into that account can open unencrypted backups.

## Protect it

- Turn on two-step verification, and have a recovery email and phone number.
- With WebDAV, some services ask to approve each new location, and backups stop until it is approved. After turning on verification, tap **Back up now** and check that the time changes.
- {app} never asks for the password of the phone's cloud account. With WebDAV, it only stores the password for that service, sealed on this phone.

## Inactivity

An account that goes unused for a long time can be deleted, depending on each service's rules. Sign in to it regularly.

## Space

If the account runs out of space, backups stop uploading. With **Drive on the phone**, {app} may not know it: that is why the cloud is checked. With WebDAV, photos stop uploading before the data backups, and **My data health** shows the space in the cloud.

If {app} warns that space is running low, free up space elsewhere, such as photos or videos, never by deleting {app} backups.

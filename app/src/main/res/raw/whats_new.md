What each version of {app} brought, newest first.

## 1.0.7

- **Producing by saying what it takes:** "made a cake with 200 grams of flour and 3 eggs" produces with that. If the product has no recipe, the card asks what it takes and, when registered, keeps it as its recipe. If it has one, it shows how what was said differs, and **Update the recipe** changes it too. "made 10 empanadas and 5 bread rolls" registers both at once.
- **New versions are announced:** once a day, with internet, {app} checks whether there is a new version, without sending any data. If there is one, a notification arrives, only once per version, and **Home** says so until it is installed. Nothing is downloaded until **Update** is tapped. It is turned off with **Check for updates automatically**, in **Updates and APK check**.
- **Notifications at the start:** at the end of the initial setup, a short sheet explains what notifications are for, and then Android asks for the permission. People who already used {app} are asked only once. If it is declined, it is not asked again: notifications are turned on in **Settings**, **Alerts**.

## 1.0.6

- **Say listens:** on phones where **Say** closed right away, it now listens. {app} asks the phone's voice service for the language it has downloaded, and tries each voice service on the phone until one works.
- **Expenses in the bar:** the bottom bar shows **Home**, **Sell**, **Expenses**, **Products** and **Numbers**. **Expenses** opens the month's expenses, with **By category** above the list.
- **Say, bottom right:** **Say** is now the round microphone button at the bottom right, on **Home**, **Expenses**, **Products** and **Numbers**. It does the same as before.
- **Understands more:** many more ways of saying each operation, numbers such as "2 thousand 500" or "50 000", the type and category of a new product ("it is an ingredient, in the raw materials category"), and a new category is created with the product. Questions, plans such as "tomorrow I buy" and denials record nothing.
- **Correcting keeps the rest:** with the card open, a sentence such as "no, the wheat flour" or "remove the bread" changes only what it names.

## 1.0.5

- **Say, in the center:** the button at the bottom center is **Say**. Tap it and speak: {app} listens with its own screen, shows the sentence as you say it and then the card. The first time it asks for the microphone. The bar shows **Home**, **Sell**, **Say**, **Products** and **Numbers**; **Records** opens from **Home**.
- **Numbers and quantities:** a dictated amount such as "50,000" is fifty thousand, and a whole quantity shows without decimals: **10 kg**, not 10,000 kg.
- **Microphone not working?** in **Say** opens the steps to make it work.

## 1.0.4

- **Say or type:** tap **Say** on **Home**, on the widget or on the icon shortcut, and say or type what happened, such as "sold 3 bread rolls and 2 juices for cash". {app} shows it on a card with each product, its amount and the total, and records it only when you tap **Register**. It understands sales, purchases, expenses, production, stock adjustments, payments, recipes, new products, and cost and price changes. Correct it by tapping a line or with a short sentence such as "no, it was 3".
- **Direct voice, optional:** in **Settings**, the phone's voice service can open with a single tap. {app} never uses the microphone.
- **Cost:** when editing a product with movements, the cost says it updates with each purchase and offers **Correct cost**.

## 1.0.3

- **Help:** 25 new articles, among them recipes and production, purchases and suppliers, expenses, categories, price lists, records and the Business hub. Help is grouped by what you want to do, and the help button of each screen opens its article.
- **Production:** without recipes, **How to create a recipe** explains the steps. Before, it opened the combos, which are not produced.
- **Products:** editing a product no longer removes the usual supplier that an import gave it.

## 1.0.2

- **Update in one tap:** when Check for update finds a new version, **Update** downloads it, checks it against the signed version notice and the {app} signature, and then Android asks to install it. The data stays.
- **Restock:** restocking a product with a recipe opens production.
- **New product:** the opening stock can be entered with its total cost.
- **Suppliers:** the notice without purchases in the Suppliers tab keeps the screen margin.

## 1.0.1

- **Backups:** if the system stopped a backup halfway or the network made it wait, the backup could stay in the cloud without its metadata. Now they are kept until the backup finishes.
- **WebDAV backups:** the connection test says when a step is slow, stops after 5 minutes without saving anything and offers to retry.
- **Numbers:** the chart no longer picks a day that has not come yet as the lowest of the period.
- **Texts:** with the phone's cloud, help and steps name the **Save to Drive** button. The time of the last backup no longer ends with a double period.

## 1.0.0

First public version. It is free and offered as is, with no warranty. It is not accounting software and does not issue invoices: its receipts are for internal use.

- **Sales and customers:** sales in a few taps with several payment methods, fees, change, tips, discounts and held sales; voids and returns with compensating records; credit, payments on account and points; cash register sessions.
- **Inventory and purchases:** products with units, variants, combos and recipes; barcode scanner; stock as a ledger of movements, with batches, expiry dates, locations, production and blind counts; purchases and suppliers with average and replacement cost.
- **Expenses and reports:** recurring expenses, money accounts, profit and margin, taxes with the rates that are entered, management summary, projected cash flow, break-even and goals. Import from CSV or a spreadsheet, and export to CSV or .xlsx.
- **Documents:** receipts, quotes, orders and deliveries and a shareable catalog, all internal, with printing on paired Bluetooth printers or through the system.
- **Backups:** automatic and verified backups to a chosen WebDAV service, or to the phone's cloud with **Save to Drive**; the last good backup is never overwritten; careful restore and **Test restore**; optional encryption with a recovery code.
- **Privacy and security:** no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics, and it works fully offline. App lock, discreet mode, audit log, what data exists and where it goes, and **Delete my data**. **Check an APK** and **Check for update**, which reads a signed version notice without sending any data.
- **Languages:** Spanish, English and Portuguese, with any currency, decimals and date and number format. Widget, quick settings tile, shortcuts, tablet layout, a demo, help inside the app, light and dark themes and nine color palettes.

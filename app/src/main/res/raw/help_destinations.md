{app} stores nothing anywhere of its own: each person chooses their cloud. Before connecting, it asks to accept the policy and the terms. Without accepting them no backup goes to the cloud, but the data and the exports keep working.

## The two options

**Service with WebDAV**, **Safer**:

- It requires opening an account with a service that offers WebDAV and entering its address, username and password in {app}. If the service offers an app password, use that one.
- {app} uploads each backup and checks that it arrived.
- One backup per day of the last week is kept, with photos.

**Drive on the phone**, **Easier**:

- It works with the cloud already on the phone, with three files that are created once.
- {app} hands off each backup, and that cloud uploads it when it can: {app} cannot know whether it arrived. It requires [checking the cloud every week](help_cloud_check).
- Three backups are kept, without photos. Photos are saved separately with **Export photos**.

## With another cloud

**Drive on the phone** only works with Drive: other clouds are not tested for this option. If that cloud offers WebDAV, connect it with **Service with WebDAV**. If not, there are [local backups](help_offsite), on a card or a USB drive.

## Privacy

Each backup carries all the business data, including customer data. Without encryption, that service can read the backups, and encryption can be turned on in **Backup encryption**. Without backups, the data is only on this phone.

The option can be changed later with **Change where to save**, in **Backup**.

Batches are for products with an expiry date or that arrive in separate shipments. Turn them on in each product with **Track by batch**. Anyone who does not use them sees nothing new.

## How they work

- Each purchase of a product with batches gets its own batch, with its code and its expiry date, if it has them.
- When selling, **Assign automatically** takes out first what expires first. The batch can also be chosen.
- **Batches and expiry dates**, in **Business**, shows what is **Expiring soon** and what is **Expired**, with its value.
- **Expiry alert** sets how many days in advance to alert.

## If something does not add up

- If more was sold than was received with a batch, the difference stays as **Unassigned batch**.
- **Remove from stock** takes an expired or damaged batch out of stock, with its reason.
- **Correct expiry date** and **Correct code** fix a mistyped entry.

Batch tracking does not change if the product already has movements.

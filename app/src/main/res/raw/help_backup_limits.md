The data lives on this phone. A backup protects it if the phone is lost or damaged. {app} makes backups on its own, after changes, and never overwrites the last good backup.

## What can be lost

What was recorded after the last backup that really arrived.

- With **Service with WebDAV**, {app} uploads and checks each backup. On Wi-Fi, usually only the last few minutes are at risk. On mobile data it can take longer, unless **Back up now** is tapped. Without internet it does not go out.
- With **Drive on the phone**, {app} hands each backup to that cloud and cannot know whether it was uploaded. If that cloud fails without warning and the phone is lost, everything after the last backup that really uploaded is lost: about 7 days or more. That is why the cloud has to be [checked every week](help_cloud_check).

## The line that matters

In **Home** and in **Backup**:

- **All saved**, with **Last backup**: only with WebDAV. The data already has a backup on the service.
- **Backup handed to Drive. Still to check that it appears.**, with **Last handoff**: only with the phone's cloud. Handed off is not the same as saved.
- **Photos: N not backed up**: there are photos or receipts that do not have a backup yet. **All saved** does not include them.
- Without internet, the backup goes out when the connection is back. On mobile data, {app} says when the next one goes out. Until then, anything new is only on the phone.
- **Over a day without a backup**: the most important warning. Connect to the internet and tap **Back up now**.
- **Backup failed**: below it, the app says what happened and what to do.
- **No backup**: no place to save was chosen. If the phone is lost, the data is lost.

## More protection

- **My data health**, in **Backup**, shows at a glance how all the protections are doing.
- A local backup, in [Local backups](help_offsite), protects the data if the cloud account is lost.
- A pinned backup is one that {app} keeps so nothing is lost: it is never deleted on its own. Use **Release backup** only when sure.
- If space runs low on the phone or in the cloud, free up space elsewhere, such as photos or videos, never by deleting {app} backups.

## What never to do

- Uninstall {app} to fix a problem: it deletes what is on the phone, which may be newer than the last backup.
- Use **Clear data** or **Clear storage** in the phone settings. **Clear cache** is safe.
- Reset the phone without confirming that there is a recent backup.
- Delete, move or rename the backups in the cloud, or the three files in the phone's cloud.
- Use {app} on two phones at the same time.
- Send backups or CSV files by chat or by email: they contain the whole business.

Instead of opening each screen, you can type or dictate what happened, such as "sold 3 bread rolls and 2 juices for cash". {app} shows what it understood on a card, and nothing is recorded until you tap **Register**.

## How to use it

1. Tap **Say**, the round microphone button at the bottom right, the widget microphone or the shortcut of the {app} icon.
2. Speak: the listening screen shows the sentence as you say it. When you finish, or when you tap **Done**, the card appears with the operation, each product with its quantity and amount, the payment and the total.
3. Check it and tap **Register**.

The first time, {app} explains what it uses the microphone for and Android asks for the permission. Without the permission, or with **Type**, the sheet opens so you can type in **What happened** or dictate with the keyboard microphone, and then tap **Understand**. To sell without a sentence, **Sell by hand**.

## What you can say

- Sales: "sold 2 kilos of rice and a dozen eggs on credit to Mary".
- Purchases: "bought 10 kilos of flour for 500 from the distributor".
- Something new, with its type and category: "bought 2 kilos of flour at 50, it's a supply, in the raw materials category". The card offers to create it as a supply in that category. If the category does not exist, the card shows it as **New category**, and it is created along with the product. **Choose category** changes it.
- Expenses: "paid the electricity 80", "spent 15 on a taxi".
- Production: "made 20 empanadas", "baked 50 bread rolls", or several at once: "made 10 empanadas and 5 bread rolls". With what went in: "made a cake with 200 grams of flour and 3 eggs".
- Stock: "2 bread rolls got damaged", "3 milks expired", "gave away a cake", "there are 40 bread rolls left".
- Payments: "Mary paid me 20", "paid Mr. Ramiro 50".
- Recipes: "the cake takes 200 grams of flour, 3 eggs and 100 grams of sugar", "to make 10 empanadas I use 1 kilo of flour".
- Products: "new product cinnamon candle at 15, cost 6".
- Cost and price: "the cost of bread is now 4", "bread now sells for 6".

You can name several products in one sentence, with numbers in digits or in words ("half a kilo", "half a dozen"). One sentence is one operation: say a sale and a purchase separately.

## Producing with what it takes

- With a recipe, "made 20 empanadas" takes the inputs from the recipe, for the quantity produced.
- Without a recipe, the card says so and asks what it takes. With the card open, "it takes 200 grams of flour and 3 eggs", or just "200 grams of flour and 3 eggs". When **Register** is tapped, it is produced and what was said becomes its recipe, per unit: with "made 2 cakes with 400 grams of flour", the recipe keeps 200 grams per cake.
- With a recipe and another quantity, like "made a cake with 250 grams of flour" when the recipe calls for 200, it is produced with what was said and the card shows what the recipe calls for. What was not named comes from the recipe, and an input the recipe does not have goes only into this batch. **Update the recipe** starts off; turned on, the recipe also keeps what was said.
- Units of the same kind convert on their own: 200 grams of a flour kept by the kilo are 0.200 kg, and the same with milliliters and liters, or with a saved package, like a box. Between different kinds, like liters of something kept by the kilo, the quantity is left to complete in a unit of the product.
- An input that does not exist is offered to be created as an input, with **Create "…"**, or another one is picked with **Choose another**. Until then, nothing is registered.
- Everything is saved together: the recipe with the production, and the productions of the same phrase. If something fails, none is left.

## If something is not right

- If a product matches several, the card shows them so you can choose. If it does not exist, the card offers to create it, or **Choose another**.
- Tap a line to open **Edit**: change the quantity or the price, **Change product** or **Remove from the card**.
- You can also correct it with a short sentence, without repeating everything: "no, it was 3", "the flour was corn", "the flour is a supply", "remove the sodas", "add 2 eggs", "cash", "no, it was a purchase".
- While the card is open, what you say adds to it or corrects it, and never clears what is already there. What you say without a verb, like "a kilo of flour" or "and 2 of sugar at 3 thousand", adds to the card's operation. A sentence of the same operation, like "I have a kilo of flour" on a count, also adds its products.
- A product already on the card is corrected with the new quantity. In a sale or a purchase, "and" or "another" adds to it: "and another 2 bread rolls". In a count, what you say is what there is.
- If you say another operation, like "sold 2 bread rolls" with a purchase open, the card asks whether to start another one: **Start another** discards the current card, and **Keep this one** discards the new sentence. "no, it was a sale" changes the operation without asking.
- At the top of the card, **Transaction** switches between sale, purchase, count and production with one tap, and the lines stay.
- If you say the type or category of a product that already exists, like "the flour is a supply", the card does not change: that is changed in the product, with **Open product**.
- A line only leaves with "remove…" or with **Remove from the card**.
- **Start over** clears the card.

Anything missing is marked in place, and **Register** saves nothing until it is complete. What you record is the same as if you had done it on its screen, and you void it as usual.

## If it does not listen

- If nothing was heard, **Listen again**, or speak closer to the phone.
- If the microphone permission was denied, the sheet offers **Open phone settings** to grant it, or you can dictate with the keyboard microphone.
- {app} listens with the phone's voice service. If the phone does not have it, or does not have the language, the keyboard remains. For the keyboard to dictate: in its settings, turn on voice typing and download the language to use it offline; in the phone settings, under apps, check that the voice service app is installed and turned on.

**Microphone not working?**, on the sheet, opens this help.

## Listen when tapping Say

It is on at first. In **Settings**, **Sales and payment**, turn off **Listen when tapping Say**: then **Say** opens the sheet to type or dictate with the keyboard. {app} uses the microphone only while the listening screen is showing and does not keep any audio. The phone's voice service turns the voice into text and may use the internet if it does not have the language downloaded.

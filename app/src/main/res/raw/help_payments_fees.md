Payment methods are the ones the business uses: cash, card, transfer, wallet or other. Create and sort them in **Settings**, in **Payment methods**. {app} only records: it does not process payments, and whoever sells checks that the money arrived.

## Each method

- **Name** and **Type**.
- **Percentage fee** and **Fixed fee**: what the payment processor charges. {app} subtracts it to show the **Net received**.
- **Days to settle**: when the money reaches the account.
- **Surcharge or discount for this method**: it applies only when charging with that method.
- **Money account**: where the money collected goes, if accounts are used.
- **QR image** and **Payment instructions**: shown at checkout, with **Show QR**.

## At checkout

In **More checkout options**, a sale can be paid with several methods: the payments must add up to the total. With cash, {app} calculates the change.

## Fees and profit

Fees lower the final profit, not the sales profit. In **Numbers**, **By payment method** shows what was collected, the fee and the net for each method.

A method with sales is not deleted: it is archived, and the recorded sales keep it.

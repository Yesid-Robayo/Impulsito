**Delete my data**, in **Storage and deletion**, is the orderly way to remove everything. It never happens on its own.

## Two scopes

- **Only this phone**: deletes the data on this phone. The backups stay where they are.
- **This phone and the backups**: first the backups in the connected destination, and last what is on this phone, so that a failure halfway through does not leave nothing at all.

Before confirming, {app} says what will be lost, offers to export and asks to type a word. With the lock turned on, it also asks for the fingerprint or PIN.

## Depending on where the backups go

- With **Service with WebDAV**, the backups are deleted from the service, which, depending on its rules, may keep them in its trash for a while. Without internet, what is on this phone is not touched while there are backups left to delete, unless that is expressly confirmed.
- With **Drive on the phone**, {app} asks that cloud to delete the three files, and cannot know whether that deletion went through: check it from another device or from the web.

## What it does not reach

It only reaches the backups in the cloud connected now. It does not reach the SD card, exported files, CSV files, backups in a cloud used before or files that {app} stopped using: the person deletes those.

After deleting this phone and its backups, the data cannot be recovered. Export it first.
